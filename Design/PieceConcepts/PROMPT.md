# Non-pawn piece shape concepts

Created using the built-in image generation tool. Orange and emerald team variants of each of six silhouettes, presented as a concept comparison sheet. The game sprites have not been replaced.

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: game piece design concept sheet for the circular strategy game Manu et Mente.
Create one polished, carefully aligned comparison sheet showing SIX non-pawn piece designs in TWO team colours. This is a flat 2D game-asset design study, not a screenshot or a 3D render.
Backdrop: solid very dark blue-teal (#0e232b).
Composition: landscape sheet, a spacious 3-column by 2-row grid of six equal cells. In each cell put TWO identical silhouettes side by side, orange on the left (#ff5c06) and emerald green on the right (#0bb352). Put the piece name centered below its pair in clean warm ivory sans-serif text. No grid lines. All shapes should have equal visual weight and comfortable spacing.
Exact reading order and exact labels:
Top left: "HAND" — a broad upright shield silhouette with gently rounded shoulders, a flat or slightly arched top, and a single downward point. One very simple dark palm-like inset is optional.
Top middle: "MIND" — a balanced upright diamond silhouette with softened corners and one small dark diamond inset.
Top right: "TECHNICIAN" — a bold mechanical gear silhouette with exactly six broad teeth and a dark circular center hole.
Bottom left: "APPRENTICE" — a broad rounded triangle pointing upward with one small dark triangular inset.
Bottom middle: "SCHOLAR" — a recognisable symmetrical open-book silhouette with two broad page wings, a center fold and a single dark center spine. Avoid fine page lines.
Bottom right: "SAGE" — a symmetrical four-point star with broad tapered points and concave sides, similar to a compass sparkle. Exactly four points, no extra rays.
Style: crisp flat vector-like graphics, saturated opaque fills, strong continuous very dark outlines, simple broad insets. Each outer silhouette MUST be visibly different. DO NOT place the silhouettes inside circles, discs, hexagons, shields behind other silhouettes, or identical token bases. The outer silhouette IS the game piece. The shield applies only to HAND. NO pawns.
Keep the original six designs consistent as one family, readable at tiny mobile-game sizes. Avoid fine detail, metallic effects, gradients, bevels, gloss, shadows, glow, texture, photorealism, decorative borders, extra symbols, logos, or additional text. This sheet should feel like professionally designed production-ready 2D strategy-game tokens.
