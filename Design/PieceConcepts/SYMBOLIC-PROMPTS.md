Put the piece name centered below its pair in clean bold warm ivory sans-serif text. No grid lines. Exact reading order and exact labels:
Top left: "HAND" — a simplified upright armored gauntlet, palm facing viewer, thumb and four broad joined fingers clearly readable. Use only a few bold dark divisions to suggest armor. The outer silhouette is the glove itself, not a shield or disc.
Top middle: "MIND" — a stylised horizontal almond-shaped eye with a bold central dark pupil and a simple orange or green iris. The outer silhouette is the eye, without an enclosing token.
Top right: "TECHNICIAN" — a recognisable broad blacksmith anvil with a flat top, one pointed horn, a narrow waist and a stable broad foot. Flat orange or green fill with dark outline. No hammer, gear or extra tools.
Bottom left: "APPRENTICE" — a compact young sprout with two broad leaves on a short thick stem and a small curved base. Simple, sturdy silhouette rather than fine botanical illustration. No pot, circle or badge.
Bottom middle: "SCHOLAR" — an open drafting compass instrument, two substantial angled legs hinged at a clear round pivot above. A simple broad crossbar may connect the legs. This is the mathematical drawing tool, not a compass rose or navigation dial. Bold, compact readable silhouette with no thin needle.
Bottom right: "SAGE" — a single upright stylised flame with one broad tapering tip, a rounded base and a simple dark teardrop inset. No candle, torch, star or additional flames.
Style: crisp flat vector-like graphics, saturated opaque fills and very dark outlines. Consistent professional mobile strategy-game asset family. Broad forms with minimal internal details, readable at tiny sizes. Each piece's outer silhouette MUST differ. DO NOT place any symbol inside circles, discs, shields, squares, or identical token bases. The outer silhouette IS the game piece. NO pawns.
Avoid gradients, metallic effects, bevels, gloss, shadows, glow, texture, photorealism, decorative borders, logos, extra symbols, additional text, or multiple variants beyond the two specified team colours per piece.
