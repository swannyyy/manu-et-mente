# Main-menu logo proposals

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. The orbital monogram (concept 2) was selected and installed in the MainMenu scene as MainMenuLogo. The production sprite is Assets/Art/Branding/OrbitalMonogram.png.

## 1. hand-and-mind

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE polished logo concept for the main menu of an original circular chess strategy game called Manu et Mente (Latin: by hand and mind). Emblem only, absolutely no text or letters. Design a bold, elegant stylized open hand whose palm contains a simple brain or intertwined thought-knot, integrated with two concentric segmented rings recalling a circular chessboard. Make hand and brain readable and sophisticated, not anatomical or medical. A few broad, balanced shapes and generous negative space; crisp flat vector-like edges. Restrained warm brass and ivory with a small jade accent, designed to stand out against a very dark slate game menu. Premium tabletop strategy identity, calm and intelligent. Centered isolated emblem, square framing, generous clear margins, truly transparent background. No mockup, no presentation board, no extra variants, no gradients, no glow, no shadows, no watermark.

## 2. orbital-monogram

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE polished logo concept for the main menu of Manu et Mente, an original circular chess strategy game. An iconic interlocking double-M monogram, two angular M forms combining into a symmetrical crown-like strategic emblem, enclosed by one precisely segmented orbital ring inspired by concentric circular chess tracks. A clever compact symbol with strong silhouette and negative space, readable as a small game icon. Emblem only: no game title, no caption, no other letters beyond the abstract double-M. Crisp flat vector-like design, warm ivory and muted brass with a restrained copper accent, excellent contrast on dark slate. Premium modern board game identity. Centered square composition, generous clear margins, truly transparent background. No mockup, no presentation sheet, no gradients, no glow, no shadows, no watermark.

## 3. circular-crown

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE refined emblem logo for the main menu of Manu et Mente, an original circular chess strategy game with rotating concentric checkerboard rings. Use a few broad geometric segments so the mark remains legible at small sizes, not a detailed diagram of an entire board. A balanced medallion with a subtly classical intellectual character, polished and distinctive. Crisp flat vector-like shapes. Ivory and warm brass, a restrained jade accent. Designed for a very dark slate game menu. Centered isolated emblem, square framing, generous clear margins, truly transparent background. No mockups, no presentation sheet, no gradients, no glow, no shadows, no watermark.
